# v1.0.0 App Spec

Purpose: make the full operations manual usable in the field without turning it into a dashboard-heavy management app.

Design language is intentionally inherited from Jim's Field Playbook: cranberry/deep red, sand/cream, muted green, Georgia headings, rounded cards, fixed bottom navigation, large touch targets and offline-first WebView assets.

The app is a reference/learning layer. GorillaDesk remains the customer, payment, job and pesticide-record system of record.
